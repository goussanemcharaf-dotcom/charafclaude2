# ESKAYLI DZ — Creative Breakdown & Visual System

**Film**: « Du budget aux clients » · Meta Ads acquisition film · 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · 100 % French
**One-line idea**: *ad spend becomes business opportunities — because someone thought about the business first.*

## 1. Strategy
| | |
|---|---|
| Audience | Algerian decision-makers who already buy (or are about to buy) Facebook / Instagram ads: e-commerce, clinics, restaurants, real estate, services, beauty, fashion, B2B, local businesses |
| Their tension | "I spend on ads. I get views. Where are the clients?" |
| Insight | Budget without strategy is noise. The same budget, routed through an understanding of the business, becomes conversations. |
| Promise | Eskayli DZ builds the Meta Ads strategy around your business, then launches, tests and optimises until attention turns into clients. |
| Proof strategy | No numbers, no testimonials, no logos. The film *shows the method* — understanding, angles, tests, optimisation — so the method is the proof. |
| Action | « Parlons de votre projet. » — one calm CTA, no urgency. |

## 2. The visual metaphor — SIGNAL → SYSTEM → CONVERSION
One **signal** — a small orange point of light, the budget — travels through the whole film without a cut in its logic:
1. it leaves the budget field of a campaign and disappears into noise (hook);
2. it hits an empty destination (« ? ») (question);
3. it is thrown into a dead-simple workflow that breaks (problem);
4. it orbits a business core and lights six satellites (understanding);
5. it snaps into a grid — Eskayli (reveal);
6. it is shaped into an angle, a message, a creative, a target (system);
7. it splits into tests A / B / C; losers fade, the winner relaunches (optimisation);
8. it flows through ATTENTION → PROSPECT → CONVERSATION → CLIENT (outcome);
9. it keeps going past « vues » into a real opportunity (big idea);
10. it becomes the dot of the brand mark (reveal + CTA).

## 3. Two worlds
- **Noise** (chapters 1–3): scattered off-grid cards, slightly rotated, a faint film grain, grey-on-black, a flicker of random ads. Nothing aligns.
- **System** (chapters 4–10): a hairline 12-column grid (1 px, 7 % white), mono metadata, everything snaps, orange flow-lines route information.
- **Brand frame** (chapter 10): one hard cut to warm off-white — the only light frame of the film.

## 4. Palette (5 colours, one accent)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0B0B0C` | background (near-black, warm) |
| Graphite | `#161618` / `#1E1E21` | panels, cards |
| Hairline | `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` | grid, borders |
| Mist | `#8C8C92` | secondary text, metadata |
| Paper | `#F4F2EE` | primary text; the brand frame |
| **Signal** | `#FF5A1F` | the budget signal, active states, conversions, the CTA — nothing else |

## 5. Typography (one family)
- **Geist 800** — headlines, kinetic words, tight tracking (−0.04 em), sentence case or all caps for system labels.
- **Geist 500/600** — UI and subtitles.
- **Geist Mono 500** — small technical metadata (campaign IDs, statuses, stages) — same superfamily.
- Hierarchy rule (from the reference grammar): small grey connector + large white keyword; **one** orange word per line at most.

## 6. Motion rules
- Easing: `outExpo` for arrivals, `inOutCubic` for camera, a 4 % overshoot only for elements that *lock* into the system.
- **Snap-to-grid**: when strategy arrives, elements travel from scattered positions to grid positions; a hairline guide flashes at the lock.
- **Signal routing**: the signal moves on orthogonal paths with rounded corners and leaves a 2 px orange trail that fades.
- **Two-beat reveal**: paired words appear ghosted (35 % opacity, 6 px blur) then solidify on the beat.
- Camera: a virtual camera over large boards — push-ins to details, lateral tracking across UI, a top-down pull-back to reveal the whole system; motion blur only on fast moves.
- Refused: spins, random zooms, glitch/RGB split, neon glow, bouncing captions, whooshes on every element.

## 7. Transitions (concept → concept, never slide → slide)
business core → client idéal (a satellite expands into a profile) → audience (the profile multiplies into a segment) → campaign (the segment docks into the campaign board) → creative (the ad slot opens) → tests (the creative splits in three) → prospect (the winning ad receives a reply) → conversation (the reply opens a thread) → client (the thread resolves into a confirmed order / appointment) → brand (the signal becomes the dot of the mark).

## 8. Sound identity
- Tempo 100 BPM (a calm, confident read sits on it); the score grows in four stages: **pulse** (sub heartbeat + ticks) → **structure** (clean kick, muted bass) → **system** (full groove, arpeggio, claps) → **conversion** (the fullest bar, chord stabs) → **resolve** (pad + the sonic logo).
- Sonic logo: a clean two-note signal (a rising fifth) — heard softly when the signal first appears, fully on « Eskayli DZ ».
- SFX: soft UI ticks on data, fine clicks on locks, low refined impacts on the four big moments, short airy moves on camera travels, one full silence on « L'objectif ? ».
- Voice first: the music is ducked by band under the voice; master −14 LUFS, ≤ −1 dBTP.

## 9. Safe zones & other ratios
Critical content lives in **y 440–1480** (inside the 1:1 crop of the 9:16 frame and clear of the Reels UI); supporting elements may use y 250–1640. The 4:5 (1080 × 1350) and 1:1 (1080 × 1080) versions are centre crops of the same frame, so nothing important is lost.
