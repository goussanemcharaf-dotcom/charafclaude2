## Direction
- French, confident, intelligent, calm, premium, conversational — never a radio voice, an aggressive salesman or a motivational speaker.
- The first sentence sets authority; the question gets **a real pause** (0.95 s); the voice grows more assured on « Chez Eskayli DZ… »; the final call is calm and certain.
- Method: 26 directed takes by section (8 sections + pickups); every phrase picked among its readings by an objective score (text accuracy, voice identity, pace, target prosody, joins) — details in `qa/voice_report.txt`.
